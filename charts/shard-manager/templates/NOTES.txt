=== ReSharder — Shard Manager ===

The ShardManagedDatabase CRD has been installed.

To verify the CRD is available:

  kubectl get crd shardmanageddatabases.resharder.io

Create your first managed shard set:

  cat <<EOF | kubectl apply -f -
  apiVersion: resharder.io/v1alpha1
  kind: ShardManagedDatabase
  metadata:
    name: my-app-db
  spec:
    maxShardSize: "50Gi"
    initialStorageSize: "10Gi"
    shards:
      - s1
      - s2
      - s3
      - s4
    dependentDeployments:
      - my-app
  EOF

Check the resource status:

  kubectl get shardmanageddatabases
  kubectl get smd  # short name
